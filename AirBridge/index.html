01 / THE EXPERIENCE

A calmer way to move your files.

↗

Send files

Select files in AirBridge and share a local link for downloading them from another device on the same network.

↙

Receive files

Open the receive page and upload files from a browser on a connected device to your Android phone.

⌗

Connect with QR

Scan a compatible AirBridge link QR code to open the connection in your browser with a confirmation step.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
Less friction.

STEP 01

Choose a direction

Pick Send Files or Receive Files in the Android app.

STEP 02

Connect your devices

Use the provided local link or QR code from a device that can reach the same network address.

STEP 03

Transfer in your browser

Download or upload through the browser page. Keep both devices on a network that allows them to communicate.
